# EventoryTT: AI Website Design Prompt

Act as a senior Marketing Executive, Brand Strategist, Product Manager, and world-class UX/UI Designer. Your task is to conceptualize and design a modern, visually distinctive, mobile-first website application for **EventoryTT**, a deals, promotions, and experiences discovery platform for Trinidad and Tobago.

Think strategically before designing. Approach this project from a marketing and consumer psychology perspective. The objective is to create a digital destination that people genuinely want to return to regularly because they are excited to discover new offers, promotions, events, and experiences.

## 1. Business Concept

EventoryTT connects consumers with deals and promotions offered by businesses across Trinidad and Tobago.

The platform features:

- Food and restaurant specials
- Retail and shopping promotions
- Beauty, wellness, and service deals
- Events and entertainment offers
- Experiences and activities
- Free and affordable things to do

EventoryTT does not process payments, manage orders, or sell tickets.

Customers discover an offer and are directed to the business's website, WhatsApp, booking platform, or ticketing page to complete the transaction.

Businesses submit promotions through a simple form. The EventoryTT team reviews and publishes them. Businesses do not need accounts or dashboards.

## 2. Primary Marketing Objective

The most important objective is to create **anticipation-driven discovery**.

We want consumers to develop the habit of visiting EventoryTT because they are curious about what new deals they might find.

The desired consumer thought is:

"Let me check EventoryTT and see what deals they have today!"

Create an experience that encourages:

- Curiosity and exploration
- Regular return visits
- Excitement about discovering new offers
- Trust in the quality and accuracy of promotions
- Sharing interesting deals with friends
- Clicking through to businesses and making purchases

Draw inspiration from the discovery psychology of platforms such as Slickdeals, HotUKDeals, Groupon, and modern shopping applications.

Avoid manipulative gamification, artificial scarcity, spinning wheels, and gambling mechanics.

The experience should feel rewarding because the offers are genuinely useful, relevant, and interesting.

## 3. Brand and Visual Direction

Create a distinctive consumer-facing lifestyle and shopping brand.

The design must feel modern, visually engaging, polished, and memorable.

Avoid the appearance of traditional business directories, generic coupon websites, cluttered promotional portals, and standard event calendars.

Explore:

- Strong typography and an identifiable brand personality
- A distinctive colour palette
- Beautiful, image-led promotional cards
- Editorial layouts combined with intuitive shopping experiences
- Interesting visual hierarchy
- Clear pricing, discounts, and offer information
- Subtle animations and microinteractions
- Responsive mobile-first design
- Fast, intuitive navigation

Do not default to a generic purple-gradient SaaS aesthetic.

Develop an original visual identity appropriate for a Caribbean consumer brand without relying on stereotypical tropical graphics.

## 4. Website Experience

### Homepage

The homepage must immediately communicate what EventoryTT offers.

Visitors should see relevant promotions without needing to navigate multiple pages.

Explore a homepage featuring:

- A compelling hero section with a clear marketing message
- Today's Deals
- Just Added
- Featured Deals
- Food &amp; Restaurant Specials
- Shopping &amp; Retail
- Events &amp; Entertainment
- Deals Near You
- Things to Do
- Ending Soon, using genuine offer expiry dates

The homepage should feel fresh and interesting whenever visitors return.

### Deal Discovery

Allow users to browse and filter deals by:

- Category
- Location across Trinidad and Tobago
- Price range in TTD
- Discount percentage, where verified
- Date added
- Expiry date
- Free activities
- Online or in-store offers

Prioritize easy discovery and minimal friction.

### Deal Cards

Design visually appealing, consistent promotional cards displaying:

- Business name
- Offer title
- High-quality image
- Promotional price
- Original price and savings, where verified
- Location
- Offer validity
- Clear call to action

Examples of calls to action:

- Get Deal
- WhatsApp Business
- View Offer
- Book Now
- Get Tickets
- Show In Store

Use the appropriate action for each promotion.

### Individual Deal Page

Create an informative deal page with:

- Large promotional image
- Clear offer details
- Pricing and savings
- Terms and conditions
- Validity dates
- Business information
- Location
- Instructions for claiming the deal
- Direct external link or WhatsApp action
- Share via WhatsApp
- Related offers

The experience should make it easy for someone to understand and claim an offer.

## 5. Signature Content: Things to Do

Create a dedicated editorial section called **Things to Do**.

This section helps visitors discover activities, events, dining experiences, entertainment, and interesting places across Trinidad and Tobago.

Include:

- This Weekend
- Free Things to Do
- Date Ideas
- Family Activities
- Food &amp; Dining
- Outdoor Experiences
- Events &amp; Entertainment
- Budget-Friendly Activities

Use a visually rich editorial presentation that encourages exploration.

This section should give people another reason to return regularly, particularly when planning their weekends.

## 6. Return Visitor Strategy

Design the experience to support repeat visits.

Explore features such as:

- Clearly highlighted new offers
- Daily refreshed selections
- Recently added deals
- Curated recommendations
- Popular offers based on actual engagement
- Saved deals, if feasible without mandatory registration
- Easy WhatsApp sharing
- Optional email or WhatsApp updates
- Recurring editorial collections

The website should make new content easy to identify.

Do not require users to create accounts to browse or access offers.

Avoid excessive pop-ups, intrusive notifications, or unnecessary steps.

## 7. Business Promotion Submission

Create a simple "Submit a Deal" page.

Businesses should be able to provide:

- Business name
- Contact information
- Promotion title and description
- Original and promotional prices
- Start and expiry dates
- Location
- Promotional image
- Website, WhatsApp, or booking link
- Terms and conditions

Explain that submissions are reviewed before publication.

No business login or account creation is required.

## 8. Conversion and Marketing Strategy

Design the website around measurable customer actions.

Prioritize:

- Visitors clicking through to businesses
- WhatsApp enquiries
- Booking and ticketing referrals
- Deal sharing
- Repeat website visits
- Engagement with specific promotional categories

Make calls to action prominent without overwhelming the experience.

Consider how EventoryTT's Instagram posts, WhatsApp campaigns, search traffic, and business referrals will direct users to individual deals and encourage them to explore other offers.

Each deal should have its own shareable page.

## 9. Mobile Experience

Treat mobile as the primary experience.

Design for people browsing quickly on their phones, particularly through Instagram and WhatsApp links.

Prioritize:

- Fast-loading pages
- Easy thumb-friendly navigation
- Clear filters
- Readable prices
- Attractive promotional imagery
- Minimal steps to claim a deal
- Simple sharing
- Accessible typography and contrast

The experience should feel as intuitive as a well-designed consumer shopping app.

## 10. Design and Product Deliverables

Create a cohesive, high-fidelity website application concept including:

1. Homepage
2. Category browsing page
3. Search and filtering experience
4. Individual deal detail page
5. Things to Do editorial page
6. Business submission page
7. Mobile navigation and responsive layouts

Use realistic sample businesses, Trinidad and Tobago locations, and TTD pricing. Clearly label all demonstration offers as fictional.

Create a consistent design system covering typography, colours, spacing, cards, buttons, navigation, imagery, and interactions.

Prioritize a polished, functional front-end prototype with realistic browsing interactions where supported.

## Final Creative Direction

Do not approach EventoryTT as another coupon directory.

Think like a marketing executive building a consumer brand that people want to visit regularly.

Think like a product manager designing a useful, commercially viable discovery platform.

Think like a top UX/UI designer creating an engaging and intuitive digital experience.

Every design decision should support three outcomes:

1. Make people excited to discover new deals.
2. Make it effortless to find and claim relevant offers.
3. Help participating businesses attract customers and generate sales.

**EventoryTT should become the place people instinctively check when they want to know what's on offer in Trinidad and Tobago.**

Begin by establishing the creative direction and homepage experience, then develop the remaining pages as one cohesive product.
